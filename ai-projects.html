Co-founder/Live product/2026

BGH Scout

By the time a role reaches the major job boards, it is usually already flooded with applicants. The people I met while job searching were not short on talent or effort — they were short on visibility into openings that had not yet surfaced.

So I built the thing that was missing: an automated job intelligence platform that scrapes employer career sites directly, every day, and puts new postings in front of people before the boards catch up. Ben Greene and Ty Holland joined to turn it into a real product.

  • 400+ employer career sites scraped daily across 14 industries.
  • 2,000+ new postings surfaced per day, delivered through a dedicated web application.
  • Scaled to 120+ active users.
  • Includes ways to identify and connect with people inside the hiring companies.
Users have landed roles through postings the platform surfaced — including opportunities at small and mid-sized companies they would never have found by refreshing the big boards.
