Independent consulting/Pharmaceutical client/2026

Medical Field Insight Classification & Recommendation Pipeline

A pharmaceutical client was collecting thousands of medical field insights a year — observations from the field that were being recorded but never systematically reviewed against strategy. The volume made manual analysis impractical, so the insight sat unused.

I designed and deployed a multi-stage AI pipeline to close that gap:

  1. An NLP classifier categorizes each field observation against the client's Strategic Medical Plan.
  2. An LLM generates ranked, citation-backed recommended actions for each strategic objective.
  3. A differential stage compares the previous cycle's recommendations against the new ones and reports what changed.

Classification accuracy improved through structured training data design, expert annotation standards, and custom stop-word management — translating client feedback directly into measurable gains rather than anecdotal tuning.

The system is in active production use. The third stage matters most in practice: it means each cycle reports change rather than restating the same recommendations.
